Privacy Policy

RoyalCub Jobs (also written Royal Cub or royalcubjobs) · Last updated: 11 November 2026

RoyalCub Jobs is a local jobs and services marketplace where clients post vacancies and hire skilled workers, and service providers create profiles and chat with clients. This policy explains what data the RoyalCub Jobs web app (the “app”, “we”, “us”) accesses, how we use, store, share and protect it, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. It applies to the app and to this website. We will update this page, and the date above, if we change how the app uses Google user data.

1. Google user data

What we access. If you choose “Continue with Google”, we receive only your Google name, email address (and whether Google has verified it) and your Google account ID (a unique identifier). We do not request or access your Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

How we use it. We use this information only to create your RoyalCub Jobs account, recognise you when you sign in again, link a Google sign-in to an existing account with the same email, and keep your account secure. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Who we share it with. We do not sell, rent, transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties, except to the service provider that runs our sign-in and account system (see section 5), as needed to provide the app, or where the law requires it.

What we never do. We do not use Google user data for targeted, personalised, retargeted or interest-based advertising; we do not sell it to data brokers or information resellers; we do not use it to determine credit-worthiness or for lending; we do not build databases from it beyond your own account; and we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalised or non-personalised artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. No human reads your Google user data unless you ask us for support and give consent, it is needed to investigate abuse or a security issue, or the law requires it.

Google API Services User Data Policy. RoyalCub Jobs’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and improve the user-facing features of RoyalCub Jobs.
  • To let you post jobs and services, search, chat and make bookings.
  • To send in-app notifications and respond to support requests.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, scams, spam and other misuse, and to enforce our Terms & Conditions.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Where data is stored

Account and app data is stored in your own browser (local storage on your device) and with our sign-in and account provider described below. Local storage stays on your device until you clear it or log out and clear site data.

5. Sharing, transfer and disclosure

We do not sell your data. We share information only in these limited cases:

  • Other users. Content you post and the profile details you make visible are shown to other users, and messages go to the person you send them to.
  • Service providers. We use Supabase to provide sign-in and account services, Google Identity Services to offer “Continue with Google”, and Google Fonts to load fonts. These providers process data only to deliver their service to us and under their own privacy terms.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the app.
  • Business change. If RoyalCub Jobs is ever transferred to another owner, your data would stay subject to this policy, and we would tell you before it became subject to a different one.

We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone for purposes other than providing or improving the features described in this policy.

6. How we protect your data

  • The app is served over HTTPS, so information is encrypted in transit.
  • Google sign-in uses Google’s own authentication; we never see or store your Google password.
  • Sign-in tokens are verified before an account session is created, and access to account data is limited by authentication rules on our account provider.
  • We limit the data we collect to what the app needs.

No system is perfectly secure. Please keep your login details private and tell us promptly through Help & support if you suspect unauthorised access.

7. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the app.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information, including any Google name, email address and account ID we hold, within 30 days, unless we must keep something longer to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute.
  • When the retention period for a type of data ends, we delete or destroy it.

How to request deletion. Open the app, go to Help & support, choose “Ask for help” and ask us to delete your account and data. You can also remove posts and your profile picture yourself from the app, and clear data stored in your browser through your browser settings.

Revoking Google access. You can stop RoyalCub Jobs from using your Google sign-in at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After you do, ask us to delete your account if you want your data removed.

8. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your profile, change who can see it and whether you appear online, and ask us for a copy, correction or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional legal rights; contact us through Help & support and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Age requirement

RoyalCub Jobs is for people aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a child has given us information, tell us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above, and if we change how the app uses Google user data we will notify users in the app before the change takes effect.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, use Help & support inside the RoyalCub Jobs app, or return to the RoyalCub Jobs home page and sign in.
