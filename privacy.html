1. Who we are

RoyalCub Jobs ("RoyalCub Jobs", "we", "us") is operated by [[OPERATOR_NAME]] and available at [[SITE_URL]]. It lets clients post vacancies and hire skilled workers nearby, and lets service providers publish a profile, offer services and chat with clients. This policy explains what personal information we handle when you use the site. RoyalCub Jobs does not process payments: users pay each other directly.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, age, email address, phone number, account category (client or type of service provider), optional company or service role, and a password if you sign up with email.
  • Profile details: a profile photo, your location as text you type, and your privacy settings.
  • Content you create: vacancies, service offers, stories, bookings, follows, chat messages, and help or report requests.
  • Records of your choices: for example, the version and time at which you accepted our Terms and Conditions.

Information from Google Sign-In

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google sends us your name, email address, whether that email is verified, and your Google account ID. We request only the basic sign-in scopes (openid, email, profile). We do not receive your Google password.

Information we do not collect

We do not access your device's GPS location, contacts, camera roll (other than a photo you choose to upload), or payment details. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Technical information

Like any website, the services that deliver our pages and sign-in (listed in section 6) receive standard request data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. Our site also stores preferences and session data in your browser's local storage (see section 7).

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show your profile, vacancies and service offers to other users so people can find and contact each other.
  • To deliver your chat messages and notifications.
  • To handle help requests, reports and safety issues, and to enforce our Terms and Conditions.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet legal obligations.

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, we rely on performing our contract with you (providing the service), our legitimate interests in running and securing it, your consent (for example, when you choose to sign in with Google), and legal obligations.

4. Google user data

Information we receive from Google (name, email address, email-verified status and Google account ID) is used only to create and sign you in to your RoyalCub Jobs account and to link that account to your Google identity. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not transfer it to others except to the service provider that runs sign-in for us (Supabase, section 6) or when required by law.

RoyalCub Jobs' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove RoyalCub Jobs' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Who sees or receives your information

  • Other users. Your name, profile photo, location, company or service role, category, vacancies, service offers, stories and the accounts you follow can be seen by other signed-in users, subject to the privacy settings you choose. Your chat messages are sent to the person you message. Your email address, phone number and age are stored with your account and are not part of the profile details we share with other users unless you put them in a post, message or profile.
  • Site administrators can review help requests and reports you submit.
  • Service providers that host and deliver the service on our behalf (section 6).
  • Authorities or others when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users or the service.
  • A successor if the service is transferred as part of a merger or sale; we would tell you first.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

7. Storage and retention

Some information is stored in your browser's local storage on your device, such as your account details for email sign-up, preferences, notifications and a local copy of content. It stays there until you clear your browser data. Shared content (posts, service offers, stories, profile details, follows and chat messages) and Google sign-in records are stored with Supabase and kept while your account is active. When you delete a post, it is removed from shared storage. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your information within 30 days, except what we must keep to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

We use browser local storage and session storage, not advertising cookies. Google may set its own cookies when you use Sign in with Google.

8. Security

Connections between your browser and our service providers use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not share government ID numbers, bank details or other sensitive information in posts or chat messages, and use a password you do not use elsewhere.

  • Control whether your profile is visible, whether your online status shows, and who can message you, in Settings.
  • Remove Google's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Ask us to access, correct, export or delete your information, or to delete your account, by writing to [[CONTACT_EMAIL]].

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to restrict or object to certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

10. Age requirement

You must be at least 16 to create an account. RoyalCub Jobs is not directed to children. If we learn that we hold information from someone under 16, we will delete it.

11. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you in the app before the changes take effect.
